CNN Forward Pass

Every tensor your SimpleCNN from Lesson 3 produces, laid out pixel by pixel — the padding ring, the kernel sliding by its stride, and each intermediate shape in between. The sample image and all weights below are fixed and untrained (random, scaled like PyTorch's default init) — this is about seeing the mechanics run, not about a trained model's accuracy.
